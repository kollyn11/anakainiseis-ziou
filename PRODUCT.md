## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, near-zero client JS), plain CSS with custom properties, hosted on Netlify (Netlify Forms), Sveltia CMS at /admin. Chosen in the owner's brief.

## Users

Homeowners and small business owners in Attica (Athens, West Attica, Elefsina) who need a renovation, painting, drywall/false ceilings, external thermal insulation (θερμοπρόσοψη) or roof/terrace waterproofing. Mostly arriving on a phone from Google or Instagram, comparing a few local tradespeople, and wanting to call or get a quote quickly.

## Product Purpose

Public landing page for ΖΙΟΥ Ανακαινίσεις (founded 2025). Success = phone calls, Viber/WhatsApp messages and quote-form submissions from the service area, plus local search visibility.

## Positioning

A hands-on craftsman, not an agency: the person who quotes is the person who does the work; clear written quotes; tidy sites; dates kept.

## Brand Commitments

The client's own logo is binding: a running stick-figure tradesman holding a paintbrush and a drill, with "ΑΝΑΚΑΙΝΙΣΕΙΣ ΖΙΟΥ / est 2025", redrawn as vector (geometry in src/assets/brand/runner-parts.mjs; static SVGs logo-runner.svg / logo-full.svg; client's original JPG in src/assets/brand/source/). Palette (approved 2026-10-18): navy #14325C, orange #E8662B, graphite #3E4A57, sand #F3F1ED / #E4DFD6, white. In the logo the figure is navy on light and white on navy; the cap, the paint on the brush and "est 2025" are orange. Primary button = orange with navy text. Commissioner typeface; squared (0–4px) corners; flat. Tagline "Χτίζουμε σωστά." The header logo runs with scroll (forward/back with direction, speed with scroll velocity, settling into the drawn pose); smooth scrolling via Lenis. The old house-roof icon and paint-roller wordmark are retired.
