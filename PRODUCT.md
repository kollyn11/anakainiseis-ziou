## Stack

## Users

Homeowners and small business owners in Attica (Athens, West Attica, Elefsina) who need a renovation, painting, drywall/false ceilings, external thermal insulation (θερμοπρόσοψη) or roof/terrace waterproofing. Mostly arriving on a phone from Google or Instagram, comparing a few local tradespeople, and wanting to call or get a quote quickly.

## Product Purpose

Public landing page for ΖΙΟΥ Ανακαινίσεις (founded 2025). Success = phone calls, Viber/WhatsApp messages and quote-form submissions from the service area, plus local search visibility.

## Positioning
